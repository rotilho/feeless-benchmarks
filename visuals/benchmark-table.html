Benchmark scope

Each implementation published 50,000 transactions across 500 independent account chains, with zero errors. Timing starts at publication and stops at the client-observed completion boundary.

Display precision

Latency is rounded to whole milliseconds here; exact nanoseconds remain in each summary and CSV. Average TPS is shown to two decimal places, and peak TPS as an integer.
